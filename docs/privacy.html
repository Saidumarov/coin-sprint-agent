Privacy Policy

Coin Sprint Agent · Last updated: October 21, 2026

Home Terms of Service

Coin Sprint Agent ("the app") is a private, single-user application operated by the owner of the Coin Sprint YouTube channel to manage that channel. This policy explains what data the app accesses and how it is used.

YouTube API Services

The app uses YouTube API Services. By using the app you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of your data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Data the app accesses

  • Channel information of the authorized channel (name, ID, public statistics).
  • Videos the owner uploads through the app, and their titles, descriptions and tags.
  • Comments on the channel's own videos: comment text, author display name and publish time.
  • Analytics of the channel's own videos (views, watch time, likes, subscribers gained).
  • OAuth tokens issued by Google so the app can act on the owner's behalf.

How the data is used

  • To upload and schedule videos approved by the channel owner.
  • To read comments and post replies on the channel's own videos.
  • To analyze the channel's own performance and improve future videos.
  • To send the owner private status reports.

The app does not sell, rent or share data with third parties and does not use it for advertising. Comment text may be processed by an AI model provider (Anthropic) solely to classify the comment and draft a reply for the channel owner.

Storage and security

Data is stored in a private database controlled by the channel owner. OAuth tokens are kept in private server configuration, are never published and are never written to logs. Comment and analytics data is retained only as long as needed to run the app and is deleted on request.

Revoking access and deleting data

You can revoke the app's access to your Google account at any time at Google security settings. To request deletion of stored data, email the contact below; it will be deleted within 30 days.

Children

The app is not directed to children and does not knowingly collect personal data from children.
