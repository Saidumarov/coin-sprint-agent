Coin Sprint Agent

A private content assistant for the Coin Sprint YouTube channel.

Privacy Policy Terms of Service

What it is

Coin Sprint Agent is a single-user application run by the owner of the Coin Sprint channel. It helps the owner produce and manage short videos for their own channel. It is not a public service and does not offer accounts to other people.

What it does with YouTube

  • Uploads Shorts that the owner has reviewed and approved, scheduled for a chosen time.
  • Reads comments on the channel's own videos and posts replies; sensitive replies are sent only after the owner approves them.
  • Reads the channel's own analytics to learn which videos perform better.

Access is granted by the channel owner through Google OAuth and can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
